# Nik Studio - example script
#
# One scene a line. Blank lines space it out. A line starting with #
# is parked - it stays here but is not made.
#
# Do NOT describe Nik or the art style here. Those are set once, in
# CHARACTER and STYLE at the top of cell 2, and are put in front of
# every line automatically - which is the only thing keeping him the
# same boy from shot to shot.
#
# What each line should say, in this order:
#   1. ONE thing he does. Not three.
#   2. Who else is in the shot, and that they stay.
#   3. Whether the camera moves. Say "the camera does not move"
#      unless you actually want it to.
#
# Anything you leave out, the model invents. That is how the puppy
# melted the first time.
#
# For a 125 second song, twelve to sixteen scenes is right. Fewer and
# the same clip comes back too often; the notebook will tell you.

He walks into a sunny flower garden and waves hello, the puppy trotting beside him, the camera does not move

He crouches down and pats the golden puppy, which wags its tail, the kitten watching from the flowers, the camera does not move

Close up of his face as he laughs, cheeks round, the garden soft behind him, the camera does not move

He claps his hands twice, the orange kitten sitting beside him, the flowers still, the camera does not move

The little yellow duckling waddles up to him and he points at it, smiling, the camera does not move

He sways from side to side, singing, the puppy and the kitten beside him, butterflies drifting past, the camera does not move

Close up of the puppy barking happily, its ears bouncing, the garden behind it, the camera does not move

He stamps his feet one at a time, the duckling copying him, the camera does not move

He holds his arms out wide and turns slowly, the animals watching, the camera does not move

Close up of the kitten meowing, whiskers twitching, flowers behind it, the camera does not move

He jumps up and down with both feet, laughing, the puppy hopping beside him, the camera does not move

He kneels and hugs the puppy gently, the kitten and duckling close by, the camera does not move

Wide shot of all four of them standing together in the flowers, he waves, the camera does not move

He blows a kiss towards us and smiles, the animals beside him, the camera does not move
