AB KYA KARNA HAI
================

Character mil gaya, framing theek ho gayi, bachche jaisa anupaat aa
gaya. Do cheezein baaki hain. Bas ye do kijiye.


KADAM 1 - CHARACTER CHUNIYE  (2 minute)
---------------------------------------
Notebook mein ye line dhoondhiye aur bhar dijiye:

      CHARACTER = "Casual_Male"

Abhi wo khaali hai, isliye "BaseCharacter" chal raha hai - wo pack ka
khaali dhaancha hai, isi liye kaala aur bina shakal ka dikhta hai.

Doosre theek option: Casual2_Male, Casual3_Male, Casual_Bald

MAT LIJIYE: Zombie, Goblin, Knight, Ninja, Pirate, Soldier, Witch,
Wizard, Viking - bachchon ke channel ke liye galat hain.

Cell 2 chalaiye. Nik.blend khud dobara banegi.


KADAM 2 - ANIMATION LIBRARY  (15 minute)
----------------------------------------
Ye sabse zaroori kaam hai. Iske bina scenes jam ke khade rehte hain.

Abhi jo pack hai wo GAME ke liye bana hai - usme death, punch, sword
slash hai. Aapke gaane ko clap, wave, nod, point, spin, sway chahiye.
44 mein se zyada lines ke paas koi movement hai hi nahi, isliye wo
'idle' par gir jati hain, aur is pack ka idle patthar jaisa hai.

  1. quaternius.com par search kijiye:

        Universal Animation Library

     120+ animation, CC0, free.

  2. Download kijiye, unzip kijiye

  3. Drive mein NAYA folder banaiye aur poori extracted folder usme
     daal dijiye:

        My Drive / NikStudio / Moves /

  4. Notebook mein:

        MOVEMENTS = "Moves"

  5. Cell 2 chalaiye


DONO KE BAAD
------------
Notebook mein ye do line honi chahiye:

      CHARACTER = "Casual_Male"
      MOVEMENTS = "Moves"

Cell 2 chalaiye aur jo 3 clips banein wo mujhe bhej dijiye.

Output mein ye line dekh lijiye - ismein wave, nod, point, sway
dikhne chahiye:

      Actions   : NN (clap, idle, jump, nod, point, ...)

Aur ye wali khaali honi chahiye:

      ! These movements have no action ...
